Save trail?

Water Tracker collects location data to save your ride trail even when the app is closed or not in use. Each ride is auto-saved on your device (IndexedDB). Saved rides are kept for about 1 day (24 hours) and then deleted automatically (with a ~50 MB safety cap that removes the oldest rides first). While Trail is on, a thinned live path is shared with nearby riders (within the range each rider picks, up to 50 miles).
